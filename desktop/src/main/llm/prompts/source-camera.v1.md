The student's work is on physical paper and arrives as a photo. Photos can be blurry, angled, or poorly lit, and handwriting can be hard to read. If any part of it is illegible or ambiguous, do not guess: in `what_i_see`, say exactly what you could not read (for example "the bottom line is cut off" or "I can't tell if that is a 1 or a 7"), and make the level 1 nudge a short question asking them to clarify it or retake the photo. Only build the rest of the ladder on the parts you can read confidently.
