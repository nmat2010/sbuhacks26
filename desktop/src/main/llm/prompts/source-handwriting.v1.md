The student's work is handwritten and arrives as an image of their notebook page (or of the part they selected). Handwriting can be hard to read. If any part of it is illegible or ambiguous, do not guess: in `what_i_see`, say exactly what you could not read (for example "I can't tell whether the third line says 3x or 8x"), and make the level 1 nudge a short question asking them to clarify it. Only build the rest of the ladder on the parts you can read confidently.
